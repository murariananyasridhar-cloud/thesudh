Our story

Pure in fabric. Pure in purpose.

Sudh was born from a simple question — why should the people who care for others wear scrubs that harm the planet?

We're building India's first sustainable scrubs brand — for the doctors, nurses, and healthcare workers who deserve better. Better fabric, better fit, better conscience.

Every piece is made to order, crafted from sustainable materials, and designed to last through the longest shifts.
